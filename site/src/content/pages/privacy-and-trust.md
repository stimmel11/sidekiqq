## The approval rule

SideKiqq never sends a message on its own. It writes drafts. A draft only goes out when you approve that specific draft:

- On your phone or watch, by tapping **Approve**.
- With glasses, by saying "send it", hearing who it's going to, and confirming.

There is exactly one path in the system that sends a message, and it requires that approval. No setting, schedule, or device can skip it.

## What it reads

When you connect your Google account, SideKiqq asks for access to:

- **Gmail**, to read incoming mail, find commitments, and prepare drafts.
- **Google Calendar**, to know what's on today and to propose meeting times.
- **Google Drive**, to look up documents you refer to.

You grant this access through Google's own sign-in screen, and you can withdraw it at any time from your Google account settings.

## What it remembers

To be useful tomorrow, SideKiqq keeps structured notes about what it has read:

- Commitments: who promised what, and when it's due.
- Open loops: things started but not finished.
- Clients and their timelines: decisions, meetings, and follow-ups.

These notes are stored in a private database used only by your SideKiqq.

## Where the thinking happens

SideKiqq's assistant runs in the cloud. To understand an email or answer a question, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic's Claude model for processing. Your devices don't do the thinking. They send your question and show or speak the answer.

Your Google sign-in credentials stay on the server, encrypted. They are never stored on your watch, glasses, or medallion.

## Your devices

- Each device is paired to your SideKiqq with a one-time code shown on your phone.
- The medallion only listens while you hold its button. It has no speaker and no screen.
- Alerts sent to your phone contain a short summary. The full detail stays in the app.

## What it labels honestly

SideKiqq separates what it knows from what it is guessing. A due date that was stated is shown as confirmed. One it worked out from context is marked as inferred, on screen and out loud.

## One person, one assistant

Each SideKiqq serves a single owner. Information about one of your clients is never used in a reply about another.

## What this page is not

This page describes how SideKiqq is designed today, while it is still in development. It is not a legal privacy policy. A full privacy policy and terms will be published before SideKiqq is available to anyone outside the team.
