## How they work together

Each device does what it is best at. The phone holds the detail. The watch is for a glance. The glasses and medallion are for asking without stopping what you're doing. The answer always comes from the same place, with the same memory of your clients and commitments.

## Approving from any device

Whatever device you use, sending a message takes an explicit yes. On the phone and watch you tap **Approve**. With glasses you say "send it", hear who it is going to, and confirm. The medallion can't send anything on its own.
